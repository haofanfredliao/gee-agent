  - Use annual median/mean of `avg_rad` as urban activity weight.
  - VIIRS starts in 2012; for earlier decades, state no VIIRS weighting is available and fall back to spectral-only extraction or calibrate with a reference year.

Reflectance and QA:
- Landsat L2 optical bands require scaling: multiply by 0.0000275 and add -0.2.
- Apply QA_PIXEL cloud/shadow/snow masking before index calculation.

## Definitions (must be explicit)

Impervious surface proxy:
- Build a transparent score, for example:
  - high NDBI (built-up tendency),
  - low NDVI (non-vegetated),
  - low MNDWI (non-water),
  - optional VIIRS weight to suppress non-urban bare land.
- Example normalized score (0-1):
  impervious_score = norm(NDBI) * (1 - norm(NDVI)) * (1 - norm(MNDWI)) * viirs_weight

Binary impervious mask:
- Apply threshold on `impervious_score` (or on a rule-based combination) to produce `impervious_mask`.
- Threshold must be printed and justified (fixed threshold or AOI-adaptive percentile).

Urban footprint area:
- impervious_area_km2(year) = sum(pixelArea where impervious_mask==1) / 1e6

Annual Growth Rate (AGR):
- For interval [t0, t1]:
  AGR_km2_per_year = (A_t1 - A_t0) / (t1 - t0)
  AGR_pct_per_year = ((A_t1 / A_t0)^(1/(t1 - t0)) - 1) * 100  # only when A_t0 > 0

Decadal comparison:
- Compute area at each decade anchor (e.g. 2000, 2010, 2020) or decade means.
- Report delta area and AGR for each decade interval (2000-2010, 2010-2020, 2020-2025 if requested).

## Method workflow

1) Time slicing:
   - Build annual (or multi-year median) Landsat composites in AOI.
   - Keep season consistent across years (e.g. same dry season months) to reduce phenology bias.

2) Impervious extraction:
   - Compute NDVI/NDBI/MNDWI from scaled reflectance.
   - Build impervious score and threshold to binary mask per year.
   - For 2012+, multiply by VIIRS-derived urban weight (bounded to reasonable range, e.g. 0.5-1.5 after normalization).

3) Area estimation:
   - For each target year/decade, calculate impervious area (km2) and share of AOI.
   - Keep reduction scale consistent (typically 30 m for Landsat-based results).

4) Trend metrics:
   - Fit linear trend of impervious area vs year (optional slope).
   - Mandatory: compute AGR for each requested interval and decade pair.

5) Validation hints (if labels unavailable):
   - Visual cross-check against high-res basemap for sample zones.
   - Compare impervious growth hotspots with increasing VIIRS intensity.

## RAG output checklist (assistant must include)

1) Data and period:
   - Landsat collections used, compositing season, year range, AOI source.
   - Whether VIIRS weighting is used and from which years.

2) Core expansion statistics:
   - Impervious area (km2) for each key year/decade.
   - Annual Growth Rate in both km2/year and %/year (where valid).
   - Net expansion and relative expansion ratio.

3) Decadal urban footprint comparison:
   - 2000s vs 2010s vs 2020s footprint changes (area delta + AGR).
   - Identify fastest-growth decade and possible spatial shift direction (if computed).

4) Caveats:
   - Spectral confusion with bare soil/construction sites.
   - Cross-sensor consistency and Landsat-7 SLC-off artifacts.
   - VIIRS unavailable before 2012 and coarse resolution mismatch vs Landsat.

## Critical rules

1) Do not claim VIIRS-weighted results for years before VIIRS availability without explicit fallback statement.
2) Do not mix unscaled and scaled Landsat reflectance in index calculations.
3) Use consistent seasonal windows when comparing across decades.
4) Always print thresholding logic and reduction scale.
5) For AGR percentage, guard against division by zero or tiny baseline area.
